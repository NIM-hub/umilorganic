What's actually in the bottle

Meet some of the ingredients behind UmiL.

No filler oils, no synthetic thickeners. Every ingredient in UmiL is chosen for what it does for hair and scalp, and we think you should know what that is.

Phyllanthus emblica

Amla

Indian gooseberry, packed with vitamin C and antioxidants — a staple in South Asian hair care for strengthening strands and supporting natural colour.

Olea europaea

Olive Oil

Our base oil — heavy in vitamin E and fatty acids that coat each strand, seal in moisture, and soften hair from root to tip.

Allium cepa

Onion

A well-known staple in traditional hair oils — its natural sulphur content is prized for supporting keratin and reducing shedding.

Coriandrum sativum

Coriander

Rich in antioxidants and gently antimicrobial, it calms an irritated or flaky scalp and helps keep the hairline clear and healthy.

Fagonia arabica

Dhamasa Booti

A desert herb long used in traditional Unani and Ayurvedic remedies for its calming, purifying properties — added here for scalp comfort.

Simmondsia chinensis

Jojoba Oil

Structurally close to your scalp's own sebum, so it absorbs quickly, balances oiliness, and carries the other herbs deeper without leaving residue.

Using it right

How to apply UmiL

1

Massage into the scalp

Work a small amount gently into your scalp using your fingertips, in slow circular motions for two to three minutes.

2

Let it rest

Leave the oil in for one to two days before washing, so the herbs have time to work into the scalp.

3

Wash as usual

Rinse with your regular shampoo. For best results, repeat two to three times a week.
